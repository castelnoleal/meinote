Why offline matters on a BPO floor

Most desk tools are another web service your IT and compliance teams have to approve, host and monitor. MeiNote is a self-contained app that lives on the workstation.

  • Customer data stays on the machineNotes are composed locally. Nothing is sent to an outside server, which keeps your data handling simple.
  • No downtime during outagesAgents keep writing notes and reading scripts while the network or a vendor platform is down.
  • Fits locked-down workstationsNo installer and no admin rights. It opens in the browser your agents already use, including on VDI.
  • Updates on your scheduleNew templates and scripts roll out as a single file you approve, not a forced cloud update.

How an enterprise engagement works

MeiNote is delivered as a service. We build it around your account's processes and keep it current as they change.

  1. Discovery

    We review your line of business, note standards, scripts and shift roster with your operations leads.

  2. Build

    We turn your process into templates, reminders and calculators, branded for your account.

  3. Rollout

    You receive per-shift builds to deploy on workstations or your intranet, plus a short walkthrough for team leads.

  4. Upkeep

    When a process or script changes, send the update and we ship a new build to every shift.
