Why offline matters on a BPO floor

Most desk tools are another web service your IT and compliance teams have to approve, host and monitor. Ours are self-contained apps that live on the workstation.

  • Customer data stays on the machineNotes are composed locally and nothing is sent to an outside server, which keeps your data handling simple.
  • Keeps working during outagesAgents keep writing notes and reading scripts while the network or a vendor platform is down.
  • Fits locked-down workstationsNo installer and no admin rights. Tools open in the browser your agents already use, including on VDI.
  • Updates on your scheduleNew templates and scripts arrive as a file you approve, not a forced cloud update.
